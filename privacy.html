Privacy policy

Heshiz Family stores what a family types into it and nothing else. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling of data.

Effective 27 September 2026. Applies to the Heshiz Family app for iOS and Android and to heshiz.com.

Who is responsible

IntroInnovation Oy, Finland, is the data controller. Questions and requests go to privacy@heshiz.com.

What we store

DataWhy

The camera is used only to scan a pairing code shown on a parent's phone. The image is read on the device and never stored or sent anywhere.

We do not collect location, contacts, messages, screen time, other apps, advertising identifiers or any data from the child's phone beyond the pairing itself.

Children

Heshiz Family is for families with children aged 7 and older. A child never creates an account and never enters personal data. A parent adds a child to the family, chooses what name to show, and pairs the child's phone. Everything about a child is entered by the parent, is visible to the parent, and is deleted by the parent.

The child's phone is read-only: it shows what is active for that child and when it ends. A child cannot change anything, cannot contact anyone through the app, and does not see other children's details beyond their names.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child without a parent's involvement. If you believe that has happened, write to privacy@heshiz.com and we will delete it.

Legal basis

Under the GDPR we process this data to provide the service you asked for (contract, Article 6(1)(b)) and, for server logs, to keep it secure (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f)). Data about a child is entered under the parent's authority as the holder of parental responsibility.

Who else sees it

Only the service providers that run the app, each bound by a data processing agreement, and only for the purpose named:

ProviderPurpose
NeonDatabase hosting.

Expo (Expo Application Services)Delivering push notifications through Apple and Google.

We never sell data and never share it for advertising. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as the family exists. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. A signed-in phone that is not used for 180 days is signed out. Server logs are kept 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the app, a parent who owns the family can open Settings and tap Delete the family. That removes the family, every child, every agreement and consequence, and signs out every phone. It cannot be undone.

To delete your own parent account and email address, or if you are not the family owner, email privacy@heshiz.com from the address you sign in with. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Removing a child from the family (Family tab, the child, Remove) stops them appearing in the app; their history stays with the family until the family is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you or your child, and you can object to processing. Write to privacy@heshiz.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi) or the authority in your own country.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Sign-in codes and device tokens are stored only as keyed hashes. Sign-in is rate-limited per address and per network.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we say so in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
